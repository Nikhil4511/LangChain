Under the sun, the cricket ground shines bright,
Players step in, ready for the fight.
The bat meets the ball with a powerful sound,
Cheers and excitement spread all around.
The bowler runs with fire in his eyes,
The batsman watches as the fast ball flies.
A six goes soaring high in the sky,
The crowd roars loudly as the ball flies by.
A fielder dives to stop the run,
Every single moment is filled with fun.
The captain leads with courage and pride,
With every decision, the team stands side by side.
Sometimes we win, sometimes we fall,
But cricket teaches us to give our all.
Through sweat and struggle, friendships grow,
On the field, determination starts to show.
From street-side games to stadium lights,
Cricket fills our hearts with dreams and delights.
Win or lose, the spirit will remain,
For cricket is more than a game—it runs through our veins.
